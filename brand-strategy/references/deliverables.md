# Stage 5 Deliverables

Read this file only before Step 9 or when writing or updating a Stage 5 packet.

The output has two layers in one file:

1. **Brand Foundation One-Page:** approximately 500 words excluding compact tables. It is the everyday decision tool.
2. **Detailed Strategy and Transfer Packet:** the evidence, reasoning, full frameworks, naming records, and downstream fields.

Do not call the detailed packet a one-pager. Do not make the short page carry source detail or the full manifesto.

## 3. Foundation-only and inherited runs

- In a foundation-only run, complete both layers, mark offer naming pending, and mark offer-name checks not applicable.
- In an inherited-foundation Offer Naming Sprint, carry the parent foundation forward unchanged beneath the Origin line from `references/naming.md`; update only Offer Naming, Stage 6 naming inputs, the date, and the relevant consistency rows.
- The Build Loop counts Stage 5 complete for the current offer only when `Replaces the Stage 4 working title` matches the working title in `4-offer-design.md`. A pending or stale title remains foundation-only.
